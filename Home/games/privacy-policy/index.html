Findon Software Games Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to games published by Findon Software, including Shikaku Room.

Our games do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Game progress, settings, achievements, and similar gameplay data may be stored locally on your device.

If a game uses platform services such as the Microsoft Store, Xbox services, cloud saves, purchases, or achievements, those services may process account, purchase, device, or achievement information according to Microsoft's own privacy terms.

We do not include third-party advertising, analytics tracking, or behavioural profiling in our games unless this policy is updated to say otherwise.

If you contact us for support, we will use the information you provide, such as your email address and message, only to respond to your request.
