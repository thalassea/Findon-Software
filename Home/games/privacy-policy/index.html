Findon Software Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026

This privacy policy applies to games published by Findon Software, including Shikaku Room.

Information we collect

Our games do not collect, sell, or share personal information directly.

Game progress, settings, achievements, preferences, and similar gameplay data may be stored locally on your device. This data is used only to provide normal game features, such as saving progress and remembering settings.

Platform services

If a game uses platform services such as the Microsoft Store, Xbox services, cloud saves, purchases, achievements, or sign-in, those services may process account, purchase, device, gameplay, or achievement information according to Microsoft's own privacy terms.

Findon Software does not control Microsoft's processing of data through Microsoft Store or Xbox services.

Advertising and analytics

Our games do not include third-party advertising, analytics tracking, or behavioural profiling unless this policy is updated to say otherwise.

Support requests

If you contact us for support, we will use the information you provide, such as your email address and message, only to respond to your request.

Support messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request, maintain business records, and protect against misuse or legal claims.

Children's privacy

Our games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our games.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
